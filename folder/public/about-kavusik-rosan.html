About

Kavusik Rosan is a final-year Computer Science Engineering student at Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology (SIET), Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. He is a Digital Marketing & SEO Specialist with expertise in Keyword Research, On-Page SEO, Technical SEO, Content Optimization, Search Analytics, Website Optimization, Organic Growth Strategies, Digital Branding, and Google Search Console fundamentals.

He is best known as the creator of Obscura Arcanum — an advanced anti-AI image protection platform that uses adversarial machine learning and multi-layer encryption to prevent unauthorized AI exploitation of digital media. Obscura Arcanum was shortlisted Top 100 nationally at both AICTE APF 2025 (AICTE Productization Fellowship) and Yukti Challenge 2025.

As HCL GUVI Campus Ambassador under Mission Upskill India, Kavusik organizes hackathons, mentors 200+ students, and leads technical initiatives. He has served as Department Newsletter & Magazine Head at SIET, published technical content on the GUVI platform, and holds an academic author profile at HBRP Publication.
